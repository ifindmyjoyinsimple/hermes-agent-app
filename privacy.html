Privacy Policy

Hermes Agent is a private assistant used only by the owner of incomingoutlook@gmail.com.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the app accesses that account's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events in order to read, organise and send email and manage calendar entries on the owner's behalf.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties. Access credentials are stored privately on the owner's own computer.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
